01 / ABOUT

Background

My research focuses on developing trustworthy, explainable, and human-centered AI for healthcare.

  • Approach: Build AI systems that are accurate, interpretable, fair, and clinically meaningful.
  • Applications: Clinical prediction, patient-level analysis, and healthcare decision support.
  • Across modalities: Electronic health records (EHRs), clinical text, structured/tabular data, biomedical signals, and medical images.
02 / RESEARCH

Research & interests

Current PhD research

  • Clinical Natural Language Processing (Clinical NLP): Developing and evaluating NLP and large language model methods for clinical and healthcare applications.
  • AI with real-world clinical data: Working with patient data from the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center (UPMC) on clinical prediction, patient-level analysis, and decision support.
  • Trustworthy & fair clinical AI: Investigating model bias, fairness, interpretability, and reliability; identifying and mitigating disparities across patient populations.
  • LLMs in healthcare: Exploring model use, evaluation, and reasoning, including how well models capture clinically meaningful information and support reliable healthcare decisions.

Prior research

  • Explainable AI for healthcare: Interpretable disease and health-risk prediction using demographic, behavioral, socioeconomic, family, and health information.
  • Fairness & bias mitigation: Investigating racial and demographic bias in medical AI, particularly skin cancer prediction, and developing more equitable models.
  • Biomedical signal analysis: EEG-based emotion recognition and consumer preference prediction; identifying important brain regions and electrodes using interpretability methods.
  • Generative AI & synthetic data: Generating and augmenting text, tabular data, and images to address limited data and class imbalance.
  • LLMs for structured clinical data: Transforming tabular clinical data into text for disease prediction, minority-class augmentation, and interpretable clinical reasoning.
  • Federated & efficient AI: Distributed learning approaches combining neural feature extraction with boosting-based prediction.

Research interests

  • Clinical AI: Clinical NLP, large language models, multimodal learning, and machine learning with real-world clinical data.
  • Trustworthy systems: Explainability, fairness, bias identification and mitigation, reliability, and clinically meaningful reasoning.
  • Methods: Generative modeling, neural networks, agentic AI and agentic modeling, synthetic data generation, and multimodal learning.
  • Data modalities: Electronic health records (EHRs), clinical text, structured/tabular data, biomedical signals, and medical images.
  • Goal: Develop clinically useful, transparent, equitable, and trustworthy AI systems that support healthcare professionals and patient-centered outcomes.
